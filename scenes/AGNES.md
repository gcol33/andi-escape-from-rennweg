As you round the corner, a familiar figure blocks your path. Agnes. "Andi! I've been looking for you." A chill runs down your spine. Those words never mean anything good. "About those MICROCLIM timesheets..."

---

---

A low grumble escapes your throat as you remember all the paperwork still waiting. Agnes cracks her knuckles. "You're not leaving until we sort this out. One way or another."
