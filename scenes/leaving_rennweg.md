The party winds down. Plates are stacked, bottles collected, the grill carefully extinguished. One by one, people drift toward the stairs, still chatting, still laughing.

---

"So..." Adrian appears at your elbow, that eternal smile somehow even brighter. "A few of us are heading to the Stiegl-Ambulanz. One last round before you abandon us for the physicists?"

---

You look back at the rooftop one final time. The fairy lights still twinkle. The cat watches from atop the railing, a silhouette against the darkening sky. It raises one paw in what might be a wave. Or a dismissal. With cats, you never really know.

---

The group gathers at the gate. Jen is already planning the route. Michi is arguing about which tram to take. Wolfgang is explaining how the pub's database could be optimized. Some things never change.

---

You step through the gate of Rennweg 14. Behind you: years of code, countless coffees, one very strange cat, and friendships that will outlast any job. Ahead: cold beer and warm company.

---

The iron gate swings shut with a familiar clang. **You escaped from Rennweg.**
