You flip through the Flora Book frantically, searching for anything that matches the succulent in front of you. The pages blur together—Latin names, dichotomous keys, illustrations of plants that all start to look the same. Crassulaceae? Aizoaceae? Something with an 'S'? None of it makes sense without context, without the field experience to connect the flat images to living reality.

---

"It's... definitely in the Asteraceae family? Or maybe Brassicaceae?" you venture, knowing even as you say it that you're just stringing random family names together. Franz shakes his head sadly, his disappointment almost palpable. "The book is useless without understanding. It's like having a dictionary but not knowing any grammar—all the words are there, but you can't form a sentence."

---

The botanists lose interest. The party continues, but you've been exposed as a fraud—someone with the tools but not the knowledge. If only you'd paid more attention on Siegrun's excursion, actually learned to identify plants instead of just nodding along while thinking about your code.
